# Academic Asia — Staff User Guide

**A complete guide for administrators and staff**

Version 1.0 · Last updated 19 June 2026

## How to use this guide

This manual explains how to use the Academic Asia system from start to finish. It is written for everyday staff with no technical background. Follow the numbered steps for each task and you will be able to manage students, schools, events, exams, and your team without any prior training.

> **Tip:** Screenshots would be helpful at the points marked *[Screenshot suggested]* throughout this guide. Capture these from your live system during onboarding.

# Part 1: System Overview

## What the application does

Academic Asia is a web-based management system for an education placement agency. It keeps all of the agency's day-to-day records in one place so that staff can manage the full journey of a student, from first enquiry through to enrolment at a school overseas.

In plain terms, the system helps you:

- Keep a complete record for every **student** (personal details, contacts, applications, visas, travel, exam results, and more).
- Maintain a directory of **schools** (mainly UK and international), including their courses, fees, entrance exams, contacts, and bank details.
- Plan and run **events** such as expos, interviews, auditions, and group entrance exams, and let students register for them.
- Manage **exam bookings** — confirm dates, assign rooms and seats, and record scores.
- Manage your **staff** team and their login accounts.

## Primary user types

Everyone who uses the system is a member of staff. The system is **staff-only** — there is no public or student-facing login. The main types of people who use it are:

- **Administrators / managers** who oversee the whole operation and manage staff accounts.
- **Consultants** who handle students, applications, and placements day to day.
- **General staff** who support operations such as events, exams, and record keeping.

## Main modules

The system is organised into these areas, all reachable from the left-hand menu:

| Module | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Dashboard** | A welcome screen with high-level summary figures. |
| **Students** | The full record for every student and their sub-records. |
| **Schools** | The directory of schools and everything about them. |
| **Events** | Expos, interviews, auditions, exams, and other events (8 types). |
| **Exams** | A central list of all exam bookings to confirm and score. |
| **Staff** | Your team members and their login accounts. |
| **Reports** | *Planned — not yet available.* |
| **Settings** | *Planned — not yet available.* |

## High-level workflow

A typical end-to-end flow through the system looks like this:

1. **A new student is added** to the Students module (often from a walk-in, referral, or event).
2. The student's **contacts, education history, and profile** are filled in.
3. The student is **registered for an event** (for example an expo or an interview day) so they can meet schools.
4. **School applications** are created against the schools the student is targeting.
5. **Exams** linked to the application are confirmed (date, room, seat) and later scored.
6. Once a place is offered, the student's **visa, travel, and pre-departure** details are tracked through to enrolment.
7. **Schools** are kept up to date in parallel so consultants always have accurate fees, courses, and contacts to work from.

# Part 2: Navigation Guide

## Logging in

1. Open the Academic Asia web address in your browser.
2. You will see the **Sign in** screen with the "AA" Academic Asia logo. *[Screenshot suggested]*
3. Enter your **email address** (for example `name@academic-asia.com`).
4. Enter your **password**.
5. Click **Sign In**.
6. If your details are correct, you are taken to the Dashboard. If not, you will see "Invalid email or password" and can try again.

Notes:

- Both fields are required. If you leave one blank you will be asked to fill both in.
- The login screen shows "Staff access only" at the bottom — only staff accounts can sign in.

### If you forget your password

1. On the Sign in screen, click **Forgot password?**.
2. Enter your email address and click **Send Reset Link**.
3. You will see a "Check your email" confirmation.
4. Open the email and click the reset link.
5. On the **New Password** screen, enter your new password and confirm it (minimum 6 characters, and both entries must match).
6. Click **Update Password**. You are signed in and taken to the Dashboard.

> If a reset link is old, it may expire. If that happens you will be returned to the login page and can request a new link.

## Dashboard overview

After logging in you land on the **Dashboard**. It shows a welcome message and four summary cards: Total Students, Active Schools, Upcoming Events, and Placement Rate. Below these is a **Recent Activity** area.

> **Important:** At present the Dashboard figures are sample/placeholder numbers and Recent Activity is a placeholder. Treat the Dashboard as a landing page, not a live reporting screen. For real numbers, open the module you need (each module has its own accurate counts at the top of its list page).

## Sidebar navigation (left menu)

The left-hand menu is your main way of moving around. It contains:

1. **Dashboard**
2. **Students**
3. **Schools**
4. **Events** — click to expand a sub-menu grouped into:
   - *Engagement & Guidance:* Expo / Fair, Seminar / Webinar, Briefing / Meeting, Reception / Social
   - *Admissions & Assessment:* Interview Day, Audition Day, Group Entrance Exam, Assessment / Scholarship
   - There is also an **All Events** link at the top of the group.
5. **Exams**
6. **Staff**
7. **Reports** *(not yet available)*
8. **Settings** *(not yet available)*

The section you are currently in is highlighted. Click **Events** to open or close its sub-menu.

## Top menu (header) navigation

Across the top of every screen you will find:

- A **search box** ("Search students, schools...") — *currently for display only; it does not perform a search yet.*
- A **notification bell** — *currently for display only; there are no notifications yet.*
- Your **profile avatar** (your initials) on the far right.

## User profile and settings area

Click your **avatar** in the top-right corner to open a small menu that shows:

- Your name
- Your email address
- Your department badge (Admin, Consultant, or General)
- A **Sign out** button

To log out, click **Sign out**. There is no separate editable profile/settings screen yet — your own details are maintained through the Staff module (see Part 3).

## Moving between modules

- Click any item in the left menu to open that module's main list.
- Within a module, click a record's name to open its detail page.
- Use the **back arrow** at the top of a detail or form screen to return to the previous list.
- "Breadcrumb" links at the top of some pages (for example *Dashboard › Schools › Edit*) let you jump back up a level.

# Part 3: Feature-by-Feature Guide

## Students

**Purpose**

Holds the complete record for every student the agency works with, plus all related information such as contacts, applications, visas, travel, and exam results.

**How to access**

Left menu → **Students**.

**How to use**

1. The Students list opens showing summary cards (Total Students, Active, New Leads, Pending) and a table of students.
2. Use the search box and filters to find a student, or click **Add Student** to create a new one.
3. Click a student's name to open their detailed profile.

**The students list**

- **Columns:** Student (name, avatar, Chinese name if any), ID (student code such as S000001), Contact (email and mobile), School (current school, course, entry year), Status, and Lead (temperature).
- **Search:** Type a name, student code, or email to filter instantly.
- **Status filter:** All Status, Active, New, Pending, Closed, Dead.
- **Lead filter:** All Leads, Cold, Warm, Hot, Very Hot.
- **Clear filters** appears when a filter is active.
- **Pagination:** 50 students per page with Previous/Next and numbered pages.

**Creating a new student**

Click **Add Student** and complete the form, which is grouped into sections:

- **Personal Information** — Surname *(required)*, First Name *(required)*, Chinese Name, Gender, Date of Birth, Nationality, Passport Type, Consultant.
- **Contact Information** — Email, Mobile (with country code, defaults to +852), Telephone, Address, Chinese Address.
- **Academic Information** — Current School, School Type, Target Course, Entry Year, Entry Month, Enrollment Date, Lead Source (Walk In / Referral / Events / Direct), Status, Placement (lead temperature), Exam Paper.
  - If Lead Source is **Referral**, a "Referral Detail" box appears.
  - If Lead Source is **Events**, an "Event" picker appears.
- **Additional Information** — "Subscribe to AA News" (on by default), "Needs Airport Pickup", and a Remarks/Notes box.

Click **Create Student**. The system gives the student a code automatically (for example S000001) and opens their profile.

**The student profile (detail page)**

The top of the page shows the student's name, status, lead badge, code, and **Edit Student** / **Delete Student** buttons, followed by Personal, Contact, and Academic cards and a row of **Quick Actions** (Send Email, New Application, New Education, New Visa, New Event App, New Travel, New Exam Result, New Resume).

Below this are three tabs:

- **Profile tab** — expandable sections for:
  - *Brief Introduction* (intended subjects, introduction text, approval status, and a record of which schools it was sent to).
  - *Resume* (English standard, interests, AA tests, school reports, external certificates, special talents, parents' input).
  - *Internal Notes* (character, strategy, parent expectations, plus sensitive medical/SEN and guardianship notes).
  - *Education* (courses, tutors, dates, hours, status).
  - *Visa Applications* (school, entry year, status, and a checklist covering passport, CAS, visa granted, appointment, etc.).
  - *Legal Documents* (passport details plus uploaded document files you can download or delete).
  - *Travel* (flights, arrival, airport pickup).
  - *Exam Results* (test dates, subjects, scores).
  - *Family & Guardian Contacts* (contact cards with relationship and priority).
- **School Applications tab** — each application to a school, with enrolment status, referral flag, scholarship type, linked event, and nested deposit and exam details. Toggles let you show or hide Archived and Suspended applications.
- **Event Applications tab** — every event the student has registered for, with type, status, and remarks.

**Available actions**

- **Create:** Add students and any sub-record (contact, application, visa, education, travel, exam result, resume entry, event application).
- **Edit:** Edit the student and every sub-record.
- **Delete:** Delete the student (also deletes their contacts) or any individual sub-record. A confirmation appears first.
- **Search / Filter:** By name, code, email, status, and lead temperature.
- **Send Email:** Opens your email program addressed to the student.
- **Approve:** Mark a student's Brief Introduction as approved.
- **Archive / Suspend:** Hide applications using the Archived and Suspended toggles.

**Important notes**

- Surname and First Name are the only required fields to create a student.
- The student code is generated automatically — you do not enter it.
- "Log Call" and "Add Note" quick-action buttons are not active yet (use the Internal Notes section instead).
- Deleting a student cannot be undone.
- There is no Export or Import on the student list at this time.

## Schools

**Purpose**

A directory of schools (mainly UK and international) used for student placements, including their full profile, courses, fees, exams, contacts, and banking details.

**How to access**

Left menu → **Schools**.

**How to use**

1. The Schools list opens with summary cards (Total Schools, Accepting Applications, Total Pupils, Total Boarders) and a table.
2. Search and filter to find a school, or click **Add School**.
3. Click a school's name to open its profile.

**The schools list**

- **Columns:** School (name, optional website link), Location (city, county, country), Type (gender plus phase), Students (pupil and boarder counts), Status (Accepting / Not Accepting), and Actions.
- **Search:** By school name.
- **Filters:** Country (England, Scotland, Wales, N. Ireland) and Type (Co-Ed, Boys, Girls).
- **Row actions menu:** View Profile, Edit School, Send Email, Call School, Delete.
- **Pagination:** 50 schools per page.

**Creating a new school**

Click **Add School** and complete the five sections:

- **Basic Information** — School Name *(required)*, Gender Type, Institution Type, Phase, Religious Affiliation, Pupil Count, Boarder Count, Boarder Age range. (If you pick Boys or Girls, a "Going Co-ed From" field appears.)
- **Contact Information** — Telephone, Fax (both default to +44), Email, Website.
- **Address** — Street, City, County, Postcode, Country.
- **Visa & Admissions** — Accepting Applications (on by default), Accepts Child Visa, Accepts General Visa, Minimum Child Visa Age.
- **Additional Information** — Keywords (comma-separated) and Remarks.

Click **Create School** to save and open the school's profile.

**The school profile (detail page)**

The header shows the school name, status, and **Edit School** / **Delete School** buttons. Information is organised into tabs, each showing a count of records:

1. **Basic Info** — full profile, contact details, address, visa/admission info, remarks, and keywords, plus Quick Actions (Visit Website, Send Email, Call School).
2. **Contacts** — staff at the school (name, position, phone, email, who they are responsible for, active/inactive).
3. **Supplementary Info** — extra notes by year and category (for example accommodation or EFL costs).
4. **Fees** — fee records by year and type (Course, Registration Fee, Deposit, Others) with amounts in GBP.
5. **Entrance Exams** — exams by entry year, type, year level, subject, and duration.
6. **Academic Results** — exam performance by year, type, grade range, and percentage.
7. **Notes** — categorised notes (School, Academic, Accommodation, Sports, Scholarship, and others).
8. **Courses** — courses offered, by category, year, and subject.
9. **Bank Details** — banking information (type, currency, bank, account holder, account number, sort code, SWIFT, IBAN).
10. **Visits** — a log of site visits, with date, contact met, notes, and outcome.

**Available actions**

- **Create / Edit / Delete:** The school itself and any record within the ten tabs, each via Add / Edit / Delete buttons.
- **Search / Filter:** By name, country, and type.
- **Send Email / Call School / Visit Website:** Quick links using the school's saved details.

**Important notes**

- School Name is the only required field.
- "Going Co-ed From" appears only for single-sex schools.
- Deleting a school cannot be undone.
- The "View Applications" quick-action button is not active yet.
- There is no Export or Import on the school list at this time.

## Events

**Purpose**

Plan and run the agency's events and let students register for them. There are eight event types in two groups: **Engagement & Guidance** (Expo / Fair, Seminar / Webinar, Briefing / Meeting, Reception / Social) and **Admissions & Assessment** (Interview Day, Audition Day, Group Entrance Exam, Assessment / Scholarship).

**How to access**

Left menu → **Events** (expand to choose a type), or **All Events** for the combined view.

**How to use**

1. **All Events** shows summary cards, quick links to each event type, and upcoming events grouped into This Week, This Month, and Upcoming.
2. Choosing a specific type (for example *Expo / Fair*) shows a filtered list for that type.
3. Click **Add Event** to create one, or click an event's name to open it.

**The event list (per type)**

- **Columns:** Event (name and legacy ID), Location, Date, Time, Schools (count), Schedules/slots (count), and an actions menu (View Details, Edit Event, Delete).
- **Search:** By event name or location.
- **Filters:** Date From and Date To.
- **Pagination:** With a "Showing X of Y events" counter.

**Creating a new event**

Click **Add Event** and complete the form. The fields shown depend on the event group:

- **Basic Information** — Category, Event Name *(required)*, Event Type, Delivery Mode (In Person / Online / Hybrid), Visibility, and Assigned Staff.
- **Date & Time** — Start/End Date, Duration (default slot length), Start/End Time.
- **Location & Capacity** — Venue, Online Meeting Link (only if Online or Hybrid), and Capacity.
- **Participating Schools** *(Engagement & Guidance only)* — pick one or more schools.
- **Admission Settings** *(Admissions & Assessment only)* — a single School, an optional Parent Event, and a **Scheduling Mode**:
  - *1 to 1 Time Slots* → opens a **Representatives** section (add each school representative with their room, availability window, and slot length).
  - *Group Sitting* → opens an **Exam Blocks** section (add each exam with subject, time, room, invigilators, capacity, allowed items, and special instructions).
  - *No Scheduling* → neither section appears.
- **Remarks** — general notes.

Click **Create Event** to save.

**The event detail page**

Shows an Upcoming/Past badge and **Edit Event** / **Delete** buttons, with tabs for:

- **Basic Info** — event details, quick info (schools, representatives, duration, assigned staff), and date/time.
- **Schools** — the participating schools (managed from the Edit page).
- **Representatives** — the school representatives and their availability (for 1-to-1 events).

For Interview Day events set to *1 to 1 Time Slots*, a **Schedule Sessions** button opens the scheduler.

**The scheduler (drag-and-drop)**

**Purpose:** Assign students into representatives' interview time slots visually.

1. Open the event and click **Schedule Sessions**.
2. The screen shows a progress counter ("X/Y scheduled"), a tab per representative, a list of **Unassigned Students** on the left, and a **time-slot grid** on the right.
3. **Drag** an unassigned student onto an empty time slot to book them.
4. **Drag** a booked student to another slot to move them.
5. **Block** a slot (mark a representative unavailable) by dragging the blocker item onto it.
6. For multi-day events, use the date arrows at the top to move between days.

A short confirmation message appears after each action (for example "Assigned [Student Name]").

**How students register for events**

Students are registered from their own profile (Students → open a student → **Event Applications** tab → **New Event Application**). In the dialog you choose the Event Type and Event, set a Status (Pending, Booked, Completed, Cancelled, or No Show), optionally pick a Representative, and enter seats requested/assigned. For music auditions you can also list pieces and composers.

**Available actions**

- **Create / Edit / Delete:** Events, their representatives, and their exam blocks.
- **Search / Filter:** By name, location, and date range.
- **Assign:** Schedule students into slots; assign a staff owner to an event.
- **Register:** Add students to an event from the student's profile.

**Important notes**

- Event Name is required; Admissions & Assessment events also require a School and a Scheduling Mode.
- Admissions & Assessment events are automatically set to Private/Invitation-only.
- Deleting an event also removes its schools, representatives, schedules, and linked student applications. This cannot be undone.
- On the detail page, schools and representatives are added through the **Edit** page rather than inline.

## Exams

**Purpose**

A single, central list of every exam booking across all students, so staff can confirm exam arrangements and record results in one place.

**How to access**

Left menu → **Exams**.

**How to use**

1. The Exam Management page shows summary cards: Pending, Confirmed, Completed, and Total.
2. Use the status tabs (All / Pending / Confirmed / Completed) to filter.
3. Work down the list, confirming details and entering scores.

**The exam table**

Each row shows the Student, exam Type, Subject, Year, the student's Preferred date, the Confirmed Date, Time, Room, Seat, Score, and Status. Some of these fields can be edited directly in the row.

**The exam workflow**

1. A new exam booking starts as **Pending**.
2. Staff confirm the date, time, room, and seat → it becomes **Confirmed**.
3. After the exam, staff enter the score → it becomes **Completed**.

**Available actions**

- **Edit / Update:** Confirm dates and enter room, seat, time, and score directly in the list.
- **Filter:** By status using the tabs.

**Important notes**

- Each exam is linked to a student (and through them, their school and the event the exam belongs to).
- Changes save as you make them, with a brief confirmation message.

## Staff

**Purpose**

Manage your team and their login accounts, including their role, access level, and contact details.

**How to access**

Left menu → **Staff**.

**How to use**

1. The Staff list shows summary cards (Admin, Consultant, General counts) and a table.
2. Search or filter by department to find someone, or click **Add New Staff**.
3. Click a name to open their detail page.

**The staff list**

- **Columns:** Name (and join date), Contact (email and mobile), Department (Admin / Consultant / General), Position, and Access (their access level).
- **Search:** By name or email.
- **Filter:** By department.

**Creating a staff account**

Click **Add New Staff** and complete:

- **Personal Information** — First Name *(required)*, Surname *(required)*, Position, Join Date.
- **Contact Information** — Email (primary) *(required)*, Secondary Email, Telephone, Mobile, Fax.
- **Login Credentials** — Password *(required, minimum 6 characters)*. The staff member signs in with their email and this password.
- **Role & Access Level** — Role (department) and Access Level.
- **Caseload** — Yearly, Weekly, and Daily case figures.
- **Remarks** — free notes.

Click **Create Staff**. This creates both the staff record and their login.

**The staff detail page**

Shows their contact information, role and access level, caseload figures (if any), and remarks, with an **Edit Staff** button.

**Available actions**

- **Create:** Add a staff member and their login in one step.
- **Edit:** Update any detail. When editing, leave the password blank to keep the existing one.
- **Search / Filter:** By name, email, and department.

**Important notes**

- First Name, Surname, Email, and Password are required when creating an account.
- There is **no delete option** for staff in the system — accounts can be edited but not removed through the interface.

# Part 4: Core Workflows

## Adding a new student

1. Left menu → **Students**.
2. Click **Add Student**.
3. Enter at least Surname and First Name.
4. Fill in contact and academic details as available.
5. Set the Status and Placement (lead temperature) if known.
6. Click **Create Student**. The system assigns a student code and opens the profile.

## Recording a student's contacts and profile

1. Open the student (Students → click the name).
2. On the **Profile** tab, expand **Family & Guardian Contacts** and click **Add** for each parent or guardian.
3. Expand **Brief Introduction**, **Resume**, and **Internal Notes** to build out the student's profile.
4. Each section saves on its own.

## Registering a student for an event

1. Open the student → **Event Applications** tab.
2. Click **New Event Application**.
3. Choose the Event Type, then the specific Event.
4. Set the Status (start with Pending).
5. For an interview/audition, pick a Representative if relevant.
6. Click **Create Entry**.

## Creating a school application for a student

1. Open the student → **School Applications** tab.
2. Click **Add** and choose the school and entry year.
3. Set the enrolment status and any scholarship or referral details.
4. Save. You can later open the application to add deposit and exam details.

## Processing exams

1. Left menu → **Exams**.
2. Filter to **Pending**.
3. Open a booking and enter the confirmed date, time, room, and seat → it becomes Confirmed.
4. After the exam, enter the Score → it becomes Completed.

## Scheduling an interview day

1. Left menu → **Events** → **Interview Day**.
2. Open (or create) the event and make sure it uses *1 to 1 Time Slots* with representatives added.
3. Click **Schedule Sessions**.
4. Drag students from the Unassigned list into each representative's time slots.
5. Use the date arrows for multi-day events.

## Adding a new school

1. Left menu → **Schools**.
2. Click **Add School**.
3. Enter at least the School Name.
4. Add contact, address, fees, courses, and admissions details.
5. Click **Create School**.

## Creating a staff user account

1. Left menu → **Staff**.
2. Click **Add New Staff**.
3. Enter First Name, Surname, Email, and a Password (6+ characters).
4. Choose the Role and Access Level.
5. Click **Create Staff**. Share the email and password with the new staff member.

## Resetting your own password

1. On the Sign in screen, click **Forgot password?**.
2. Enter your email and click **Send Reset Link**.
3. Open the email, click the link, set and confirm a new password, then click **Update Password**.

# Part 5: User Roles & Permissions

The system records two things about each staff member: their **Department** (their broad role) and their **Access Level** (an intended permission tier).

## Departments

| Department | Typical use |
| --- | --- |
| **Admin** | Administrators and managers running the operation. |
| **Consultant** | Staff handling students, applications, and placements. |
| **General** | Operational and support staff. |

## Access levels

| Access Level | Intended Access | Intended Capabilities |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Super Admin** | Full system access | Everything, including managing staff |
| **Manager** | Department management | Manage a department's work |
| **Senior Staff** | Extended permissions | Broad day-to-day access |
| **Staff** | Standard permissions | Standard day-to-day work |
| **Junior Staff** | Limited permissions | Limited day-to-day work |
| **Basic** | Read-only access | View information only |

## How permissions work today — important

Please read this carefully, because the current behaviour differs from the labels above:

- **Roles and access levels are recorded and displayed, but they are not yet enforced inside the application.**
- The only real gate is **logging in**. Once a staff member is signed in, they can reach every module and can create, edit, and delete records, regardless of the access level shown on their account.
- This means a person marked "Basic / read-only" can, in practice, still change and delete data today.

Until role enforcement is added, treat access levels as a **statement of intent and responsibility**, and rely on staff training and trust. Be especially careful about who is given an account, since deletions (of students, schools, and events) cannot be undone.

# Part 7: Known Limitations

These items are either unfinished or behave in a way that could confuse new users. They are listed so staff are not caught out.

**Not yet available**

- **Reports** and **Settings** menu items lead nowhere — these areas are planned but not built.
- The **Dashboard** figures (Total Students, Active Schools, and so on) are sample numbers, and **Recent Activity** is a placeholder. Do not rely on the Dashboard for real figures; use each module's own counts.
- The header **search box** and **notification bell** are for display only and do not do anything yet.

**Permissions**

- **Access levels are not enforced.** Any signed-in user can edit and delete data regardless of their assigned role (see Part 5).

**Per-module gaps**

- **Students:** the "Log Call" and "Add Note" quick buttons are inactive. There is no bulk Export or Import.
- **Schools:** the "View Applications" quick button is inactive. There is no bulk Export or Import.
- **Events:** on the event detail page, schools and representatives are added from the **Edit** page rather than directly on the detail tabs.
- **Staff:** there is **no way to delete a staff account** from the interface — accounts can only be edited.

**Things that cannot be undone**

- Deleting a **student** (which also deletes their contacts), a **school**, or an **event** (which also removes its schools, representatives, schedules, and linked applications) is permanent. Always double-check before confirming a deletion.

**General**

- The system is **staff-only**. There is no student or public login.
- Most fields are optional. Required fields are marked with a red asterisk; the few hard requirements are noted throughout this guide (for example a student needs a Surname and First Name, a school needs a Name, and a staff account needs a name, email, and password).
